## What Makes an Analyst? 🧐

- They solve puzzles 🧩 with data.
- They look for patterns 🔍 and trends 📈.
- They tell stories 📖 that help people make decisions.

---

## Core Skills Across All Analyst Roles 🌟

### Analysts are curious explorers 🧭

- Asking "why?"
- Asking "what if?"
- "I wonder…"

### Analysts are storytellers 📢

- Communicate their findings
- Create compelling visuals
- Make people care

### Analysts are problem-solvers 🛠️

- Clean up messy data
- Use the correct tools for the job
- Everything is "figureoutable"

### Analysts are clear communicators 🗣️

## Types of Analysts 📊

> **No wrong answers — truly!** For each scenario, brainstorm the questions and metrics you'd want to track.

### Sales Analyst 📈

The Sales Manager reached out to ask you to create a dashboard and metrics they might want to track to understand how sales are performing.

- Sales are divided into 4 regions: North, South, East, West
- There are 40 total salespeople — 10 in each territory
- Sales are based on $100/seat
- Management has set the quarterly sales goal at $5M per quarter

<details>
<summary>💡 Ideas</summary>

**Framing as a question:**

- What are the total sales?
- What are the total sales by region?
- What are the total sales by rep?
- What are the total sales by rep by region?

**Sales performance:**

- Total Sales Revenue (monthly, quarterly, YTD)
- Revenue vs. Target (progress toward goal)
- Average Deal Size
- Sales by Region or Territory

**Sales funnel and pipeline metrics:**

- Lead-to-Customer Conversion Rate
- Opportunity Stages (visual funnel showing deals at each stage)
- Total Value of Pipeline
- Sales Cycle Length (time from lead to close)
- Lead Sources Performance (which channels are converting the most)

</details>

### Marketing Analyst 🎨

The Marketing Manager reached out to ask you to come up with metrics they might want to track to understand: **are our marketing campaigns working?**

- The marketing team uses Google Ads to market to potential customers
- Their monthly ad spend is $10,000
- They have tracking that can tell them if an ad converts someone to a customer

### Product Analyst 💻

A Product Manager reached out to ask you to come up with metrics they might want to track to understand: **are users using the product?**

- Domo's Magic ETL is a drag-and-drop tool for data transformation that allows users to clean, blend, and prepare data for analysis without writing code
- Usage/click data is stored in a data warehouse hosted by Snowflake

<details>
<summary>💡 Ideas</summary>

**Acquisition:**

- New users — daily, weekly, monthly
- Where are users coming from?

**Engagement:**

- How often? DAU/MAU ratio
- Who is using it? Top users?
- Who has used it recently?

**Retention:**

- Cohorts: new users by week — what is your 12-week retention rate?
- Example: 20% 12-week retention

</details>

### People Analyst 🕵️

The HR Manager reached out to ask you to come up with metrics they might want to track to understand: **what is our employee headcount and retention by various segments?**

- There are 500 employees across 8 states
- The goal is to grow to 600 by next year
- There are 5 departments: Sales, Marketing, Product, HR, and Finance

### Financial Analyst 💰

The CFO reached out to ask you to come up with metrics they might want to track to understand: **are we profitable?**

- There are 2 line items they sell: software and services
- They use NetSuite for their financial software
- Expenses are updated by the 15th of each month for the prior month's books
